Credits

PCEStudio stands on other people's work. Here is whose, and what we took. If we missed someone or got a detail wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

WhoWhat we useLink
turboxrayThe idea of putting an interpreter in the ROM, which is how the browser export works. HuTrack, the HuC6280 sound engine and DefleMask converter inside the exported ROMs (MIT). The generic PCEAS framework that HuTrack's example uses. A Mesen 2 sprite sheet script, used with his permission to test our sprites against a second emulator.github.com/Turboxray/HuTrack
ppu.toys (Liora Labs)The idea and the shape of the studio, and parts of its code (MIT), used with the author's blessing.ppu.toys
MednafenOur sound chip model is derived from the PSG core of Mednafen (GPL-2.0-or-later). Mednafen is also the emulator we check exported ROMs against.mednafen.github.io
Blip_Buffer, Shay GreenThe band-limited sound buffer our PSG output uses (LGPL-2.1-or-later).—
FengariLua running in your browser (MIT).fengari.io
CodeMirror, Marijn Haverbeke and contributorsThe code editor (MIT).codemirror.net
PCEAS and its lineageThe PC Engine assembler, used to build the interpreter and the sound engine, derived from the MagicKit assembler; later changes by Ulrich Hecht (2014, Simplified BSD). Its licence text is kept beside our copy.—
llvm-mosThe LLVM-based 6502 compiler whose PC Engine target builds the 32 KiB interpreter image inside every exported ROM, and the compiled C route's output. It is not in this repository; the build scripts point at an installed copy.llvm-mos.org
The PC Engine development community (pce-devel)HuC and HuCC, the C compiler and assembler kit that the PCEAS assembler comes from (maintained by Elmer; its documentation was written by Flame, MrJ, Kaminari and Newsdee); Etripator, a PC Engine disassembler (GPL-3) that guides our ROM viewer; mednafenPceDev, the community Mednafen with PC Engine fixes; and PCE_Soundtest (MIT), a test of the differences between the HuC6280 chips. We read and learn from these; we only list one here once we really use it.github.com/pce-devel
Furnace and DefleMaskThe DMF song format our reader follows, and the way their trackers work, which guides our tracker plan.Furnace
BrullovThe Oak Woods pack and its character, used in the OAK WOODS demo on this website only. It is not part of the repository and not covered by its licence.brullov.art
AnokolisaAction Pack – CITY, used in the CITY demo on this website only. It is not part of the repository.patreon.com/Anokolisa
