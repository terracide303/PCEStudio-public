What the export is

A patch: the six channels sound together and hold (there is no note sequence here — the tracker is the other half of #35). The .pce is an 8 KiB HuCard that writes the PSG registers at reset and loops; Mednafen plays it and tests/check-wave.js compares that recording with the model's PCM sample for sample.

Balance and main volume: high nibble left, low nibble right, 15 = full and 0 = silent. Values are 1.5 dB apart (HuC6280 PSG manual PB-3/PB-4).
