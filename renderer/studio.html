Palettes 0–15 are the BAT's 4-bit palette field;
              palettes 16–31 are the sprite palettes. Entry 0 of a background
              palette (dashed) is the shared backdrop. Each swatch's tooltip
              carries its CRAM address and 9-bit word.

          

          

            

          

          

            
THE PSG IS ITS OWN CARD

            
The PC Engine's sound chip is modelled in
              renderer/pce-psg.js, proven against the hardware
              tests in tests/check-psg.js. Toys have no sound
              surface yet: that is card #71. note() already
              returns the PSG's 12-bit F word, so the vocabulary is in place.

          

          

            

            
A PCE line is an RCR interrupt, not HDMA: one entry
              per line, and the ISR must fit the blank. The compiler prices
              this (#32); the browser calls raster(line) for all 224 lines.

          

          

            

          

          

            

            
The PC Engine has no HDMA: per-line changes are the
              RCR interrupt. The compiler half is #31; machine code and the
              HuCard export are the second half of #75.
