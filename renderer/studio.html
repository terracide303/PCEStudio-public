ASSETS

The toy's files beside main.lua, in ppu.toys' shape: tiles.json (8×8 tiles, 4-bit colour numbers), palettes.json (32 palettes of 16 VCE words), sprites.json (the SAT) and waves.json (the PSG's channels). They are editable here and travel in the toy's one JSON file; the importer (#117) loads tiles, sprites and palettes into the running toy's VRAM, CRAM and SAT before its code runs, and a toy may still write its own with vram[]/pal().

R1 is the one master volume: a 4-bit level
                per side, 15 full and 0 silent. There is no per-line volume
                register and no echo register to fold — the PSG's nine
                registers are all in the voice detail's registers tab.
