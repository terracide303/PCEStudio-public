Resources

The compilers, emulators, disassemblers, trackers and documents PCEStudio learns from and builds with. Each entry says what the thing is for, whether the studio uses it today or plans to, and what its licence says.

We link to these projects and learn from them; we do not copy from a source whose licence is not stated. A licence line of not stated means exactly that: we found no licence for it in docs/REFERENCES.md, so nothing of it goes into the repository. unchecked means the entry came from a quick web search and has not been confirmed.

Code

HuC / HuCC

The C compiler kit for the PC Engine: HuCC is the current compiler, and the kit keeps the older HuC for projects already written for it.

Planned The studio has no HuCC route yet. A plain project folder in the layout HuCC and PCEAS users recognise, with a starter kit beside it, is the plan.

Licence: an aggregate LICENSE that says much of it has no stated terms

PCEAS

The PC Engine assembler inside that kit: it turns HuC6280 assembly into the banks a HuCard ROM runs from.

Today The 32 KiB interpreter image in every exported ROM and the HuTrack sound engine are built with PCEAS. Its licence text is kept beside our copy.

Licence: The legal status of much of the kit cannot be stated exactly; the MagicKit assembler's source says "This program is freeware. You are free to distribute, use and modify it as you wish."; Ulrich Hecht's 2014 changes are Simplified BSD

llvm-mos unchecked

A family of LLVM-based compilers for 6502-family chips, with a PC Engine target.

Today It builds the interpreter image inside every exported ROM and the output of the C route. The build scripts look for an installed copy; it is not in this repository.

Licence: not stated

Emulators and debuggers

Mednafen

A multi-system emulator with a PC Engine core, and the reference we compare our ROMs against.

Today Exported ROM captures are checked against it, and the studio's sound chip model is derived from its PSG core.

Licence: not stated

mednafenPceDev

A community build of Mednafen with PC Engine accuracy fixes: scanline rendering, overscan and display resolution.

Planned It is the better reference emulator for the CPU trace and frame comparisons the CPU work needs.

Licence: Mednafen's own

Mesen 2 and MesenCE unchecked

A multi-system emulator with a debugger that also covers the PC Engine; MesenCE is the community continuation after the original Mesen 2 repository was reported archived.

Planned A second-emulator sprite check, and a way to study how a ROM animates its tiles and palettes.

Licence: not stated

Disassembly

Etripator

A PC Engine disassembler: it extracts code, graphics and interrupt vectors, and can read label and comment files and CD images.

Planned It guides the studio's ROM viewer and disassembler.

Licence: GPL-3 (LICENCE.txt)

Sound

HuTrack

A HuC6280 sound engine and DefleMask converter, written by turboxray.

Today The exported ROMs play through it. A vendored copy lives under vendor/hutrack with its notice.

Licence: MIT, copyright Turboxray

Furnace unchecked

A multi-system tracker; its design and its instrument handling inform the studio's tracker plan.

Today The studio reads DefleMask modules, the kind of song these trackers write; a fuller tracker is planned.

Licence: not stated

DefleMask unchecked

A multi-system tracker with HuC6280 support; its .dmf modules are what HuTrack's converter reads.

Today A .dmf song is converted for an exported ROM, and the studio's own DMF reader reads the same format.

Licence: not stated

Huzak unchecked

A converter in development that turns a DefleMask .dmf module into a .pce ROM with its own driver.

Planned It is read for the tracker survey. It is not publicly available.

Licence: not stated

Documentation

The HuCC documentation

The community handbook for HuC and HuCC: getting started, the compiler's internals, the function reference, and pages on graphics, sound, input, memory and the System Card.

Who wrote it. Flame (aeriform) wrote the gitbook and its getting-started chapters. MrJ rewrote the function reference, Kaminari corrected and reorganised it, and Newsdee wrote newsdee.com/hucc and the wiki. Elmer maintains HuC and HuCC and merges documentation changes.

Planned It is the reference for comparing our toy API with HuCC's and for the starter kit. The rewritten function reference is in the doc-changes branch.

Licence: Licence of the documents is still not stated

Art and maps

Aseprite unchecked

A pixel-art and animation editor.

Planned It is one of the usual pixel-art tools in this scene and a likely source of the images a project imports; no path is specific to it yet.

Licence: not stated

Tiled unchecked

A tile-map editor.

Planned A map editor in HuCC's metatile format with Tiled import is one of the openings the project is considering.

Licence: not stated
